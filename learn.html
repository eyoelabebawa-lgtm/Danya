Key Endgame Principles from Danya

Core wisdom distilled from his masterclasses to elevate your endgame precision and board vision.

1. The King is an Active Piece

In the endgame, safety concerns shift. The king is no longer a passive liability hiding behind pawns; it is a powerful attacking piece that must step forward into the center.

"Activate your monarch early."

2. Opposition & Key Squares

Mastering opposition allows you to outflank the opposing king, break through defensive barriers, and shepherd your passed pawns safely to promotion.

"Control the critical geometry."

3. Prophylaxis & Patience

Endgames demand calm calculation and anticipating opponent counterplay. Rushing creates weaknesses; methodical progression guarantees conversion.

"Precision over speed."
